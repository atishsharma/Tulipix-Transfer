The short version

  • No accountThere is nothing to sign up for. We hold no list of users, because there are none.
  • No servers of oursYour files go from your phone to your computer, and nowhere else. We run nothing that receives them.
  • No trackingNo analytics and no ads of ours. On the computer a crash report is a local file that leaves only if you press Report.

What stays on your own devices

Nearly everything. The apps are built so that your library never has to leave the machines you own.

What moves between your phone and your computer

When you pair, the two devices talk directly over your own network. The link uses TLS. The phone accepts the computer's certificate only if it matches the fingerprint recorded at pairing, and the computer recognises the phone by the certificate the phone made. No other certificate is trusted, not even one that looks valid.

Only what you ask for travels:

  • files, links, text and the clipboard you send from the phone;
  • photos and videos, if you switch on camera backup (the computer checks what it already has so nothing is sent twice);
  • paper scans, receipts, voice notes, journal entries and shopping items from the Capture tab;
  • from the computer: library lists, cover pictures, streams for playback, files it offers you, and what is playing;
  • Direct download only bank text messages that look like payments, if you switch on Bank SMS. This is off by default, and the Google Play version does not have it.

You can unpair at any time from the phone or from the computer's Companion section. A removed phone is refused at once.

Google's ML Kit and Play services components, which do the translating and the scanning, may send Google performance and diagnostic information, as Google describes in its ML Kit data disclosure. They do not receive your pictures, files or lyrics.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, rent or share your personal data, because we never receive it.
  • We do not show ads or build profiles.
  • We do not read your contacts, calls or location. A photo's location is read from the picture on your phone, only to show where it was taken, and stays there.
  • We do not upload your crashes, logs or usage in the background.

Security

  • Pinned TLS between phone and computer, with a pairing code or PIN and a banner on the computer when a new phone pairs.
  • The phone's key lives in the Android Keystore; the app's data is excluded from Android backups.
  • The media proxy on the phone listens on the phone only, behind a random token.
  • On the computer: optional PIN, fingerprint or security key; database encryption; an encrypted Papers vault; helper programs checked against published checksums.

More on the security page, including how to report a problem.

How long data is kept, and how to remove it

We keep nothing, so there is nothing for us to delete. What is on your devices stays until you remove it. Your data shows how to clear the phone, unpair, and clear the computer, step by step. Files you sent to the computer are your files in the folders you chose; removing the app does not delete them.

Your rights

In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you the right to know what is done with your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to have grievances heard, and to name someone to act for you. In the EEA and the UK the GDPR gives similar rights, including access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your data-protection authority.

Because the apps process your data on your own devices and we hold none, you exercise most of these directly: you can see, export and delete everything yourself. If you think we hold something about you anyway, write to us and we will say what, and fix it.

Children
