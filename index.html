Questions

Frequently asked

Is Tulipix just a music player?

No. Music is one section. Tulipix also covers video, photos, books, finance, transfer, tools, cloud storage, journal, kitchen, feeds and papers.

How can one app contain so many features without becoming overwhelming?

Tulipix uses modular sections. Users can show, hide or turn sections off, reorder them, and choose a launch section. Presets provide quick starting configurations.

What is the difference between a hidden and an off section?

Hidden removes a section from normal navigation. Off goes further: the section can stop its background work as well.

What are the four design languages?

Material You-inspired, Glassmorphic, Original Tulipix and Neumorphic. Each can be paired with Light, Dark or OLED.

Does Pro lock my data?

The intended product philosophy is that core local data remains yours. Pro is for advanced functionality, not for holding your files hostage.
