Every library you own, in one window.

Tulipix is a private desktop app for your photos, films, music, podcasts, books, recipes, receipts and money. Fourteen sections, each with its own SQLite file on your disk. No accounts, no telemetry and no webview.

Fourteen sections

A whole shelf of apps, one sidebar.

Privacy

Your library stays on your disk.

There is no Tulipix server to send anything to. The network is used only for things you asked for, such as a poster lookup, a stream or a feed refresh. Everything else happens on your machine.

    Encrypted databases

    Turn on encryption in Settings › Security. It covers what Tulipix knows about your files. The files themselves are never touched.

    SQLCipher

    App lock

    A PIN, your fingerprint or a security key. The lock screen shows a slideshow and what is playing.

    fprintd · FIDO

    Papers vault

    IDs and documents sealed behind the app PIN. It locks again after five minutes.

    ChaCha20-Poly1305

    Verified downloads

    Every bundled tool and AI model is checked against a pinned SHA-256 hash before it runs.

    binaries.toml · ai-models.toml

    Sandboxed plugins

    Extensions run in WASM or Lua and can do only what their capability allows. Anything not listed is denied.

    wasmtime · mlua

    Crash reports stay local

    A crash writes a report to your disk. Report opens a pre-filled issue in your browser for you to read first.

    Inside

    A Flutter face on a Rust core.

    The window, the sections and the players are Flutter. The scanners, databases, metadata and media work are in Rust, in eighteen crates. flutter_rust_bridge generates the code that connects them.

    app_flutter Dart

    Shell, sidebar, design tokens, the four design languages, one page and controller per section, libmpv playback through media_kit.

    command ↓↑ event

    tulipix-bridge flutter_rust_bridge 2.13

    One file per section, api/<section>.rs: its state, its commands, and an event stream back to Dart.

    Domain crates Rust · Tokio · sqlx

    1. Rust keeps the section's session: which tab, which item, which page.
    2. Dart sends a command, such as PhotosCmd.albumNewSmart(…).
    3. Rust does the work and emits an event on the section's stream.
    4. The controller asks for a fresh snapshot and redraws.

      Is it really a one-time payment?

      Yes. You pay once and the licence never expires. Lite includes every 1.x update. Pro includes every future version.

      Do I need an account?

      No. You get a licence key, not an account, and your library stays in local databases on your computer.

      Can I move my licence to a new computer?

      Yes. Lite covers 2 computers at a time and Pro covers 5. Moving to a new one frees the old slot.

      Is this checkout real?

      No. This page uses a demo checkout. No payment is taken, nothing is sent anywhere, and the licence key it shows is an example.
