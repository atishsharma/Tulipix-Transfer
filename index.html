Why use Tulipix Transfer?

Designed for Seamless Local Sharing

Built directly into the Tulipix app, our local transfer technology bypasses the internet to deliver speeds up to 200x faster than Bluetooth.

Blazing Fast Speed

Transfers happen over a direct Wi-Fi connection between devices, reaching speeds up to 40MB/s. Share a full movie in seconds.

Zero Data Usage

No internet connection? No problem. Tulipix Transfer creates its own secure local network, so it won't consume your mobile data.

Any File, Any Size

From a single high-res photo to entire folders of uncompressed video or complex project files. There are no size limits.

Cross-Platform (Coming Soon)

Currently optimized for mobile-to-mobile within the app, we are expanding to support PC and Mac local transfers soon.

Secure Connection

Every transfer is encrypted end-to-end over the local network. Your files never touch a cloud server during local transfer.

Original Quality

Unlike messaging apps that compress your media, Tulipix Transfer sends everything in its absolute original, uncompressed quality.

Privacy First

Safe, Secure, and Strictly Local

When you use Tulipix Transfer, your files never leave the room. The app establishes a direct, temporary Wi-Fi Direct or hotspot connection between the two devices.

  • No Servers Involved Your private photos and documents are not routed through our servers or the cloud.
  • WPA2 Encryption The local connection is secured with standard Wi-Fi encryption protocols.
  • Explicit Consent Transfers only initiate after the receiver explicitly accepts the connection request.
